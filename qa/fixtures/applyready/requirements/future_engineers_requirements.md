# Future Engineers Scholarship

Future Engineers Scholarship - Official Application Requirements

Organization: Future Engineers Foundation
Deadline: October 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM Pacific Time

Applicants must submit all of the following:
1. A current resume in PDF format.
2. An unofficial transcript.
3. An original essay between 400 and 500 words answering: "How will engineering help you improve your community?"
4. One recommendation letter from a teacher, counselor, employer, or community leader.
5. A combined PDF packet named exactly LastName_FirstName_2026.pdf.

Eligibility:
- Applicant must be enrolled in high school or college during the 2026 academic year.
- Applicant must have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0.
- Engineering, computer science, technology, or related majors are preferred.

Optional:
- Applicants may include a one-page project portfolio.

The scholarship committee may request clarification if names, email addresses, school information, or graduation dates are inconsistent across submitted materials.
